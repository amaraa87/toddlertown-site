Toddler Town GameFree for iPhone and iPad · no ads · no purchases

A little town to explore, one tap at a time.

Toddler Town Game is a calm 3D town for children aged 5 and under. Tap the ground and she walks there; tap a slide, a swing, a bicycle, a ball, a tree, a kitten or a puppy and she goes over and plays. Ride the bus to the park, press the crossing button, buy a drink or some bread with toy coins, and watch the slow little cars that always stop and beep well before they reach her. There are no menus, no scores, no timers and nothing to lose, and the sounds are soft. The game makes no network requests and has no ads, purchases, account or links, so it is safe to hand over.
