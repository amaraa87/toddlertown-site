How the game works, answers for parents, and how to reach us.

What is Toddler Town Game?

A small, calm 3D town for children aged 5 and under, played on iPhone or iPad held sideways. Your child guides a little girl around one open town block with houses, a bakery, a shop, a park with a play tower and swings, a bus stop and a few quiet streets. There are no levels, scores, timers, lives or ways to lose. Every sound is a short, soft effect made by the game itself; there is no music or narration.

How to play

  • Tap the ground and she walks there. For a spot far away she runs, and she hops over low garden fences on the way.
  • Hold a finger on the screen and she follows it.
  • Tap something and she walks over and uses it: climb and go down the slide, swing, kick a ball, shake a tree, sit on a bench, pet the kitten or the puppy, or press the button at a crossing and wait for the green walking figure.
  • Bicycles: tap a bike to ride it along the paths and ring the bell. Tap her to get off.
  • The bus: tap it at the stop to get on, ride to the park and back, and press the big red stop button. After the first ride, later rides can be skipped.
  • Toy coins: she always has a few toy coins, and more lie around the town. Use them for a drink from the vending machine (water, juice or tea), bread from the bakery, or a bus fare. With no coins the bus is still free, and at the vending machine the game simply points to the nearest coin.
  • Cars drive slowly along the roads. They always slow down, stop well before reaching her and beep. A friendly police car may stop beside her and point to the pavement, then drive away.

There are no buttons, menus or words to read. The camera follows her by itself, and big palm touches are ignored, so small hands resting on the screen do not get in the way.

Questions from parents

What ages is it for?

Children aged 5 and under. It is listed in the Kids category of the App Store and rated 4+. There is nothing to read except the number on the coin pocket.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The game never connects to the internet, so it plays the same on a plane or with Wi-Fi switched off.

Does it cost anything? Are there ads or purchases?

No. The game is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no account. The coins are toy coins found in the town and can never be bought with real money. The app has no links out of the game, so a child cannot reach a website or the App Store from inside it.

Does it save progress? How do I delete it?

The game does not save progress. Each time it opens, the town starts fresh and the coin pocket is full again. It remembers only a tiny setting on the device, for example that the first bus ride has happened, so later rides can be skipped.

To clear that, delete the app from the Home Screen and reinstall it from the App Store. Nothing is stored anywhere else, and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later. The game plays in landscape (held sideways) only.

The sound is very quiet.

The sounds are kept soft on purpose, with nothing loud or sudden. Check that the device is not on silent and turn the volume up.
