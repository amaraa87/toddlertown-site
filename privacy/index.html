Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

The short version: Toddler Town Game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no account, no ads, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases and no links, and it never connects to the internet. Everything stays on the device.

This policy covers the iOS app Toddler Town Game (shown on the Home Screen as “Toddler Town”, “the app”) and this website. The app is made and operated by Amarsanaa Jargal, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). Contact: amarsanaa@outlook.com.

1. A game for young children

The app is made for children aged 5 and under and is offered in the Kids category of the App Store. We do not collect, use or disclose personal information from children or from anyone else. This means the app has nothing to report, share or delete under children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children's provisions of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (often called GDPR-K), and it follows Apple's App Store rules for the Kids category.

The app does not ask for a name, age, email address, photo, voice, location or any other detail. It does not use the camera, microphone or location services, and it has no chat, sharing or social features.

2. No network requests

The app contains no code that sends or receives data over the internet. It does not contact our servers (we have none) or any third party, and it works the same with Wi-Fi and mobile data switched off.

3. No ads, analytics, tracking or purchases

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no crash-reporting services and no third-party code that collects data. We do not track anyone across apps or websites, build profiles, or sell or share personal data. There are no in-app purchases: the coins in the game are toy coins found around the town and can never be bought. The app has no links to websites or other apps, no sign-in, and no rating or sharing prompts, so a child cannot leave the app or spend money from inside it.

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. That sharing is controlled by you and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

4. What stays on the device

The game does not save progress between sessions. It keeps only a couple of small settings in the app's private storage on the device, for example whether the child has already taken the first bus ride, so later rides can be skipped. The toy coins start again at every launch and are not stored. None of this leaves the device and none of it identifies anyone.

If you back up the device with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup can include the app's small settings. That backup is handled by Apple under your Apple account, not by us.

5. Deleting data

Delete the app. This removes everything the app has stored (except copies in any device backup you keep). Because we receive nothing, we hold no data to delete on our side.

6. Questions and parents' requests

Parents and anyone else can ask us about this policy by emailing amarsanaa@outlook.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you and delete it when the conversation is finished.

7. This website

This website uses no cookies, no scripts and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date, and describe any change to what the app does with data in its update notes. We will not start collecting personal information from children.
